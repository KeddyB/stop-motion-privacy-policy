Our Privacy Promise

What happens on your device, stays on your device.

Keddy Stop Motion Workshop is designed to be completely private. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, videos, or photos. The app operates entirely offline and does not connect to the internet to share your information.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect any personal information, usage data, or analytics. Your projects, images, and audio files belong exclusively to you. Because the app operates completely offline, it is physically impossible for us to access your data.

2. Device Permissions

To function correctly, Keddy Stop Motion Workshop requires specific device permissions. These permissions are strictly used to enable the core features of the app and are never used to track you or collect data.

📷 Camera Permission

Required to capture the individual photographs that make up your stop motion animations. The camera is only active when you are actively shooting a frame in the studio.

🖼️ Storage / Media Permissions

Required to save your finished animations to your gallery, and to import existing photos or audio files into your projects. We only access the files you explicitly select or the ones generated by the app.

🎤 Microphone Permission (Record Audio)

Required if you choose to record custom voiceovers or sound effects directly within the app's timeline. Audio is saved locally to your device and never uploaded.

⚙️ Foreground Service

Used strictly to continue rendering and exporting your high-quality videos even if you temporarily switch to another app.

3. Third-Party Access

Since we do not collect any data, we do not share any data with third parties. There are no advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or cloud synchronization services built into the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data or connect to the internet, we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your device permissions are used, please contact the Keddy development team.
